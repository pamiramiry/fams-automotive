Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does an oil change cost in Mississauga?

It depends on your engine and the oil your manufacturer specifies. Call Fam's Automotive with your year, make and model and we will give you the exact price on the phone before you come in.

Do you service Volkswagen and Audi DSG transmissions?

Yes. We do DSG (dual-clutch) fluid and filter services on Volkswagen and Audi models, as well as fluid services on conventional automatics and CVTs, using fluid that meets the factory specification.

Will you do any work without asking me first?

No. We inspect your car, tell you what it needs and what it costs, and only start once you approve. If something can safely wait, we will tell you.

Will servicing my car at an independent shop void my warranty?

Routine maintenance does not have to be done at the dealer to keep a factory warranty, as long as it is done on schedule with parts and fluids that meet the manufacturer's specification. We use spec-correct parts and give you an itemized invoice for your records.

My check engine light is on. Is it safe to drive?

A steady light usually means book a diagnostic soon. A flashing light usually means a misfire that can damage the catalytic converter, so drive as little as possible and call us.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are best, so call or book online. Quick jobs like oil changes can often be fitted in the same day.
