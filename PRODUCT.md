# Product

Fam's Automotive Inc., Mississauga, ON (address, phone and hours still to confirm: see TO-CONFIRM.md).

## What it is
Family-owned general repair shop. Reviews keep returning to three things: honest, no upselling, fair prices. Named tech in reviews: Ahmad.

## The one job
Phone calls first, quote requests second. Every section ends in a call or quote action.

## Who visits
Mississauga drivers who have been burned or upsold before and want a mechanic they don't have to second-guess. VW/Audi owners looking for DSG service without dealer prices. Mostly on phones.

## Persuasion spine
Fear (getting taken for a ride) -> promise (price before the wrench, only what it needs, family-run) -> proof (reviews, in customers' own words) -> risk reversal (nothing starts until you approve) -> call.

## Design world
Pine green + cream paper + tomato signal. Familjen Grotesk (display and body) with Instrument Serif italic for the one emotional word per headline. Hero: centred copy over a near-invisible drafting sheet: lamp glow, faint grid, a cross-drilled brake rotor in hairlines turning very slowly.
Deliberately unlike Fast Auto Centre (near-black + amber, condensed stencil type, roll-up door, video), which is close by.

## Never
- aggregateRating / review stars in schema (self-serving review markup).
- Invented review text. Truncated reviews end in an ellipsis, never a made-up ending.
- The Ahmad Safi review (same first name as the tech) is not quoted.
