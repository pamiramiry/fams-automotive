(06) The cost of waiting

Small noises turn into big bills.

It’s almost never the whole car. It’s usually one part, and it’s cheapest the week you first notice it.

Call while it’s a small job
  • A squeal when you brakeGrinding metal on metal, then new rotors as well as pads
  • Ticking on a cold startA bigger exhaust manifold leak, sometimes broken studs
  • A flashing check engine lightA damaged catalytic converter
  • Shifts getting harsh or slowA gearbox repair instead of a fluid service
  • An overdue oil changeSludge and extra engine wear
